ESPO Genius Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version. ESPO Genius runs in your own browser. What you type about an IEP, your benefits, a care plan or a will stays on your device. There is no account, and there are no ads or trackers. ESPO Genius is free.

What stays on your device

  • What you type or paste. Each tool does its work inside your browser. Your answers are not sent to us.
  • Your saved work. If a tool saves your progress, it is saved in this browser. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.
  • Photos you scan. On browsers that can read text from a photo, the reading happens on your device. The photo is not uploaded to us.

Talking to it

"Walk me through it, out loud" uses the voice typing and read-aloud that are built into your browser. Some browsers send your voice to the company that makes the browser to turn it into text. That is between you and your browser; we do not receive the audio. If you would rather not use it, type instead.

What we collect

Nothing from inside the tools. There is no account, no sign-up and no payment.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No trackers and no analytics inside the tools.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information. We do not have any to share.

Who else is involved

  • Vercel hosts the pages. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser asks Google for the font files when a page loads.

Not legal, medical or financial advice

ESPO Genius explains paperwork in plain English. It is not a lawyer, a doctor or a financial advisor, and nothing it shows is advice about your own situation. Check anything that matters with a qualified professional before you act on it.

Children

ESPO Genius is meant for adults. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.
